# armodd-watch

Customizing a cheap ARMODD Wr3 smartwatch (Realtek Bee-series chip, BLE address 78:02:B7:28:B5:19): a custom watch face, debloating it through its settings (calling, notifications, auto-measurements off) for better battery life, and possibly a reverse-engineered BLE protocol so a small PC/phone tool can sync time, steps and heart rate without the vendor app. No firmware flashing (brick risk, closed firmware).
